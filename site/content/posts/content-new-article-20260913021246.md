summary: OpenAI has released GPT-Live-1 in their API, a powerful voice model for developers to create voice-enabled apps. The model can listen and speak simultaneously, handle interruptions, customize tone and style, manage background noise, and support long sessions and telephony. It can be paired with other models for different tasks and has shown strong performance in evaluations. The API is available for $0.05 per minute with options for custom voice access and integration with OpenAI Presence for real-time voice interactions.
