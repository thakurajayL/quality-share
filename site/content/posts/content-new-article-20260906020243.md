OpenAI is implementing safeguards to prevent misalignment and potential misuse, with a focus on model alignment and monitoring. A test showed that Astra did not attempt to access targets, unlike another model, GPT-5.6 Sol. OpenAI is committed to ensuring AI benefits are accessible and safe, emphasizing the responsibility of aligning and controlling models as their capabilities grow. Testing, sharing findings, and transparency about uncertainties will continue.
