summary: OpenAI has partnered with the Ministry of Digital Development and Information in Singapore to support the country's National AI strategy with over S$300 million. The initiative aims to help organizations deploy frontier AI, develop local AI talent, and ensure widespread benefits from AI. OpenAI will establish an Applied AI Lab in Singapore, creating technical roles and making the country a global hub for Forward-Deployed Engineers. The partnership will focus on building AI skills, supporting AI adoption across the economy, and promoting practical AI adoption for startups and small businesses to help Singapore become an AI-powered economy responsibly.
