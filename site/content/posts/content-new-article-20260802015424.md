summary: Univé, a large cooperative insurer in the Netherlands, is leveraging ChatGPT to build an AI-ready workforce. They have activated 97% of ChatGPT Enterprise licenses, with 85% of users active weekly. Employees have created 1,500 custom GPTs to streamline processes like pet insurance claims and underwriting. Univé's approach focuses on leadership, governance, and employee empowerment to drive AI adoption and innovation. Their goal is to integrate AI into everyday work processes to improve services, create value, and allow professionals to focus on tasks requiring human judgement.
