summary: Microsoft and OpenAI have extended their partnership agreement to provide clarity and flexibility. Microsoft will remain OpenAI's primary cloud partner, with products launching first on Azure. Microsoft will have a non-exclusive license to OpenAI's intellectual property until 2032, with adjusted revenue share payments. The partnership will continue to focus on advancing and scaling AI for people and organizations worldwide.
