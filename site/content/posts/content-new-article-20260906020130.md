summary: OpenAI has launched Daybreak for Frontline Defenders, a $1 billion initiative to provide subsidized access to AI cyber capabilities for frontline defenders protecting essential services. The program offers training, technical support, and partnerships to help organizations defend against cyber attacks, with a focus on essential service operators in the United States and internationally. The goal is to empower resource-constrained defenders to identify and fix vulnerabilities in critical systems, ultimately making critical services more secure and resilient.
