summary: OpenAI has partnered with the PORTS-Pike project in Ohio to secure 8 gigawatts-IT of energy, creating jobs and investing in the local community. The project will prioritize local workers and businesses, provide benefits to Ohio college students, and use sustainable cooling systems. NVIDIA will invest in the project and provide support, with the data center exclusively hosting their AI compute infrastructure. OpenAI aims to establish the data center as a model for future supercomputer design, aligning with their mission to ensure AI benefits all of humanity.
