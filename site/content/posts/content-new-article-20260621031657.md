summary: OpenAI has introduced the OpenAI Partner Network, a program for global partners to develop and implement AI solutions. The network offers access to advanced models, strategy guidance, secure integration, and specialized training. Partners can advance through tiers and earn specializations in key areas, with a focus on responsible deployment and change management. The program also includes a Forward Deployed Experts initiative for complex enterprise projects, with the goal of expanding the use of AI in businesses globally.
