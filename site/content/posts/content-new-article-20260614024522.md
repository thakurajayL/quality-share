summary: OpenAI has announced their support for the EU Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-generated content, focusing on providing context and provenance for AI-generated content online. They have been working on strengthening provenance standards since 2024 and are committed to contributing to a trustworthy AI ecosystem in Europe. OpenAI's approach includes adding metadata and watermarks to images, developing verification tools, and collaborating with coalitions like C2PA to advance provenance techniques and build a safer digital ecosystem.
