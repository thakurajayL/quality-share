summary: OpenAI has collaborated with industry partners to develop Multipath Reliable Connection (MRC), a protocol that improves GPU networking performance and resilience in large training clusters. MRC allows for more efficient and reliable data transfer, reducing network congestion and failures. By utilizing MRC, OpenAI has built highly resilient networks for training large AI models, enabling faster and more predictable performance even in the presence of failures. The MRC specification is available through the Open Compute Project (OCP) for broader industry use.
