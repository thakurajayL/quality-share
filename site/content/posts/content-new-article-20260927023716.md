summary: Meta believes that AI glasses are the best way to integrate AI into daily life, but advanced AI features require larger models that cannot fit into glasses. To address this, Meta has developed Private Processing, a confidential computing infrastructure for AI workloads that ensures data privacy and security by executing AI models inside confidential virtual machines in the cloud. This system is built on Trusted Execution Environments (TEEs) that enforce data confidentiality, integrity, and code integrity. Private Processing for AI glasses involves decoupling identity, remote attestation, secure processing, and stateful memory storage within the TEEs. The system addresses the limitations of traditional cloud architectures in handling intensive AI workloads while maintaining data privacy and security. Meta's Bug Bounty program has been expanded to cover Private Processing on AI glasses, and the future of AI on glasses will rely on Private Processing for stateful, multimodal, and agentic capabilities while maintaining strict security and privacy boundaries.
