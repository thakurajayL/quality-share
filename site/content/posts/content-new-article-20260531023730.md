summary: MUFG is collaborating with OpenAI to implement ChatGPT Enterprise for 35,000 employees at Mitsubishi UFJ Bank, aiming to revolutionize operations and customer experiences. The company views AI as a tool to enhance human capabilities and creativity, integrating it into daily work routines. OpenAI has assisted in training employees, developing custom GPTs, and cultivating AI advocates within each department. Early outcomes include increased training engagement, custom GPT creation, and reduced workloads. MUFG is also exploring AI applications in customer interactions, such as wealth management apps and robo-advisory services, to offer personalized financial advice and improve accessibility to finance through AI-driven conversations.
