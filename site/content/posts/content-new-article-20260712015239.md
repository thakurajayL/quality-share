summary: GPT-Live is a new generation of voice models that enhance AI conversations by providing a more natural and intelligent experience. It uses a full-duplex architecture, delegates tasks for deeper reasoning, and offers smarter answers, better listening capabilities, visual responses, and safety features. It is being rolled out globally to ChatGPT users with different models available for different user tiers, optimized for popular languages but with limitations in accent and fluency. GPT-Live does not support voice with video or screen sharing at launch, but legacy versions of ChatGPT Voice with these features are still available.
