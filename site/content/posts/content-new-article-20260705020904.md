summary: The rapid advancement in AI model capabilities and training dataset sizes has led to quicker releases of new AI models. Efficient storage access is crucial for AI innovation, as storage bottlenecks can impact GPU performance and research speed. Meta has developed a BLOB-storage architecture to maximize GPU utilization and research velocity, focusing on unified metadata schema, regional deployment, and handling spikes and hot spots. By eliminating data proxies and implementing distributed data caches, Meta has improved latency and efficiency in AI workloads. The optimized BLOB-storage stack at Meta addresses issues like storage node lag, egress spikes, and data ingestion delays, allowing researchers to iterate on training jobs faster. Future work includes scaling storage to network limits, supporting checkpointing at higher scales, and addressing challenges in inference workloads.
