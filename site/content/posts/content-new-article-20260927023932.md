summary: Grab and OpenAI have teamed up to launch GO Forward with AI in Southeast Asia, a skilling program for 30,000 Grab partners to build practical AI skills. The initiative will start in Singapore and expand to other countries in the region, offering hands-on workshops to help participants use AI tools like ChatGPT for smarter business decisions and growth opportunities. The goal is to make AI more accessible and meaningful for the communities served by Grab and OpenAI.
