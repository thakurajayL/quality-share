summary: ChatGPT for Financial Services is a specialized ChatGPT Work experience that combines financial data with GPT-6 Astra's reasoning to assist financial services teams in developing research, financial models, and client materials. The product includes premium data from various providers, offers enterprise security and governance controls, streamlined data connections, and improved performance. It prioritizes protecting material non-public information and client confidentiality, allowing financial institutions to build valuation models, research notes, and pitchbooks in their firm's format and style. OpenAI collaborates with financial services firms to provide specialized AI solutions through their API, and eligible institutions can access ChatGPT for Financial Services by contacting OpenAI or their account team.
