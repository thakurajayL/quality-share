summary: OpenAI is developing Project Camellia, a datacenter project in Effingham County, Georgia, that will not raise electricity rates or use much water. The project will provide $80 million in community benefits, create jobs, and offer Codex credits to Georgia students. An annual audit will ensure accountability. OpenAI is working with local communities and partners to shape the project, which aims to accelerate AI development, create jobs, and strengthen domestic industry. More information can be found at www.projectcamellia.com.
