summary: Health in ChatGPT is a new feature for U.S. users that allows them to securely connect their health information to the platform. The platform prioritizes privacy and security, giving users control over their information and the ability to disconnect at any time. While ChatGPT does not replace medical professionals, it aims to empower users to take a more active role in their health by providing clear explanations and recognizing when professional care may be necessary. Users can review and update their health information, ask health-related questions, and manage their data through the Health section of the platform.
