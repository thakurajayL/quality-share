summary: OpenAI and the Government of Malta have partnered to offer ChatGPT Plus to all Maltese citizens as part of the AI for All initiative. The program aims to enhance AI literacy skills among citizens through a course developed by the University of Malta, enabling them to use AI responsibly in their daily lives. This initiative, launching in May, is the first of its kind in Europe and highlights the increasing importance of AI adoption at a national level.
