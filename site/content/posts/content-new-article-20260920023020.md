summary: Workers are using AI to perform tasks outside of their usual job roles, leading to the integration of these tasks into their regular workflows. This trend, known as "task crossover," is reshaping jobs before their titles change, with workers revisiting these cross-occupation tasks over time. The study emphasizes the importance of work design and access to AI tools in organizations' AI strategies. The recurrence of cross-occupation tasks varies depending on the type of work, with some tasks showing higher return rates than others. This research sheds light on how AI is altering the division of labor and its impact on workers, businesses, and the economy.
