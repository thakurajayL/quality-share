summary: The GPT-Rosalind series has been updated to provide greater intelligence for life sciences research, particularly in drug discovery areas like medicinal chemistry and genomics. The model has shown improved performance in various research tasks and is available for preview to eligible organizations. A benchmark called LifeSciBench has been created to evaluate the model's impact in life sciences research. GPT-Rosalind excels in medicinal chemistry, genomics, and lab work evaluations, outperforming GPT-5.5 in various tasks while using fewer tokens. It offers plugins for life sciences research and NGS analysis, interactive viewers for biological file types, and support for organizations conducting scientific research. Novo Nordisk is using GPT-Rosalind to accelerate medical research, and the model is continuously being improved to support drug discovery, translational medicine, public health, and biodefense efforts.
