summary: The text discusses the importance of privacy controls and the role of privacy-aware infrastructure (PAI) in classifying assets to enforce retention, access, and sharing policies. Challenges include noisy signals and evolving requirements, addressed through a hybrid approach involving human review and AI models. The process involves semantic annotation, ownership metadata, and a decision funnel for routing assets. Quality control measures, stable rules, and a self-regulating tuning controller are emphasized. The system aims to balance automation with human oversight for accurate and reliable classification, with future directions including expanding to other workflows and applying techniques beyond privacy. AI-native products are driving advancements in privacy-aware infrastructure, with asset classification playing a key role in privacy enforcement. The text concludes by highlighting the importance of clear contracts, rich context, and using LLMs for novelty and ambiguity in creating consistent, debuggable, and auditable decisions.
