summary: Two new reports show that organizations are increasingly using AI for execution rather than just assistance, with frontier firms leading the way in generating more output per user. The reports highlight the shift towards agentic AI, the gap between frontier and typical firms, and the importance of connecting AI agents to company context and tools. Early-career employees are using AI more than senior employees, and frontier firms are utilizing advanced capabilities more frequently. The reports stress the need for continuous employee learning, shared workflows, data infrastructure, and governance to support broader and deeper AI adoption in organizations.
