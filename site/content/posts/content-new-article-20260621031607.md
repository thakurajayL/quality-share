summary: Researchers used an AI reasoning model to reanalyze 376 unsolved cases of rare genetic diseases, resulting in a 4.8% increase in diagnostic yield. The study demonstrates how AI can assist in generating leads for difficult cases, providing evidence-linked hypotheses for specialists to review. The model does not diagnose patients but aids in identifying correct diagnoses and potential novel explanations for conditions. The study highlights the potential of AI in aiding physicians in diagnosing rare genetic diseases and emphasizes the importance of privacy and security in clinical deployment. The Manton Center will lead further research to develop a genetics AI copilot for analyzing rare disease cases.
