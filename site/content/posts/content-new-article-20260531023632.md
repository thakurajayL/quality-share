summary: SilverTorch is a new recommendation system that integrates all retrieval components for user-generated content into a single neural network, improving efficiency, quality, and engineering velocity. It operates under the Index as Model paradigm, enabling higher throughput, lower cost, and better recommendations compared to traditional systems. The system uses a Bloom index and fused Int8 ANN search to reduce memory usage and improve serving performance, while also supporting neural reranking and multi-task scoring for better recommendation quality. SilverTorch is designed for scalability and real-time updates, breaking down the wall between infrastructure and modeling for a better user experience. The system was accepted as a full research paper at SIGIR 2026, with collaboration from individuals and partner teams across Meta.
