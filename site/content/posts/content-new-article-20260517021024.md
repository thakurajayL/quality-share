summary: Codex is a tool that helps business operations teams consolidate scattered information and input into decision-ready briefs, updates, packets, and models. It streamlines the process of creating executive-ready briefs, strategic updates, decision packets, progress updates, and scenario models by organizing and analyzing relevant information. Codex can be used for diagnosing off-track initiatives, providing recurring updates, preparing decision packets, creating progress updates, and comparing strategic paths with clear assumptions, tradeoffs, risks, ownership, and impact.
