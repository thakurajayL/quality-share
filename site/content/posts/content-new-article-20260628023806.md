summary: Netflix is using AI in video editing with models Vera and VOID to improve manual editing tasks. Vera generates edit layers while preserving original footage, while VOID focuses on object removal. The models outperform existing baselines in content preservation and instruction compliance. Training data was created to support model development, and research papers detailing the algorithms have been publicly released. VOID specifically excels in preserving scene dynamics and producing realistic motion, but limitations still exist. The goal is to make complex video editing more controllable and accessible for artists, focusing on member and creator needs.
