summary: OpenAI has received FedRAMP 20x Moderate authorization for ChatGPT Enterprise and API Platform, allowing U.S. government agencies to access advanced AI capabilities securely. This milestone enables agencies to use AI for various tasks while prioritizing security, privacy, and governance. The authorization process was a collaborative effort with the FedRAMP team to balance speed and rigor. Agencies can now access OpenAI's managed AI products, including powerful models like GPT-5.5, for internal, operational, and mission-support use cases. The authorization also streamlines the evaluation process for agencies through reusable data and access to OpenAI's products in the FedRAMP Marketplace. OpenAI will continue to enhance features and work with government customers to meet their AI needs.
