summary: Meta has announced the development of Petal, a subsea cable system that will provide petabit capacity between France and the United States by 2029. The project will utilize multi-core fiber technology to double capacity without increasing power or physical infrastructure. Developed in collaboration with NEC, Sumitomo Electric Industries, and Orange, Petal represents a significant advancement in subsea capacity and will help meet the increasing demand for reliable connectivity in Europe.
