summary: OpenAI Presence is a product that helps enterprises deploy reliable AI agents for tasks like customer support, sales, and internal workflows. It combines model reasoning with policies and escalation rules to ensure accuracy and performance. Each deployment is tailored to a specific job, with the agent only receiving necessary knowledge and system access. OpenAI works closely with customers to identify workflows, connect knowledge and systems, establish permissions and policies, test the agent, and bring it into production. Presence supports real-time experiences across voice and chat and can adapt to changing customer behavior. It is not yet available as a self-serve product and is led by OpenAI Forward Deployed Engineers and select global systems integrators.
