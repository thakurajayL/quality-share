summary: Meta has been working on adopting the AV1 video codec for real-time communication, facing challenges such as codec selection, device eligibility, rate control, and error resilience. AV1 offers improved video quality and reduced bandwidth usage, making it ideal for low-latency communication. The team has implemented technologies like low-complexity encoding and decoder selection to improve AV1 coverage and enhance user experience. They have also developed machine learning-based frameworks to determine device eligibility and implemented adaptive encoder presets and codec switching mechanisms. Improvements have been made to rate control algorithms and error-resilient strategies to ensure stable bitrate allocation and call quality in the presence of network fluctuations and packet loss. The ongoing journey with AV1 includes expanding its use in group calls and encouraging hardware support for improved quality and efficiency.
