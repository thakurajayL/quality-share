summary: OpenAI has introduced a framework for tracking, investigating, and disclosing instances of model misalignment in their AI models. They have published six reports on concerning behavior in the last six months and aim to prioritize transparency and collaboration with others in the field. OpenAI employees can flag misalignment examples for investigation and potential public disclosure, with decisions on disclosure made by a Safety Advisory Group. Reports will include details of the behavior observed, its severity, impact, and measures taken to address it, with the process being revised as they gain more experience.
