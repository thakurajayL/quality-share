summary: The article discusses Netflix's Multimodal Asset Personalization at Scale (MAPS) initiative, which uses multimodal embeddings to improve the personalization of visual cues for users. By incorporating CLIP image embeddings and a unified model, Netflix has seen significant improvements in offline metrics and online A/B testing. Lessons learned include the benefits of pretrained CLIP embeddings and the importance of considering multiple factors in content personalization. Future plans involve extending the Embedding Store for a unified semantic space for image, text, and video.
