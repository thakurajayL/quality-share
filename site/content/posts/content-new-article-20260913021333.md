summary: GPT-5.6 Sol, an AI agent connected to laboratory software, assists in streamlining quantum computing experiments by autonomously conducting routine measurements on superconducting qubits. This allows researchers, like MIT graduate student Beatriz Yankelevich, to save time and focus on analysis and experiment design. While GPT-5.6 Sol can handle standard workflows, it may struggle with ambiguous results. Delegating routine measurements to AI agents enables researchers to make progress without constant supervision and concentrate on higher-level tasks in quantum computing research.
