summary: Sarah Friar shares five lessons for CFOs on redesigning finance functions around artificial intelligence based on her experience at OpenAI. She emphasizes the importance of real-time finance, building AI tools for tasks like forecasting and closing, empowering teams to experiment with AI, redesigning workflows around AI capabilities, and measuring the ROI of AI implementation. The key takeaway is that AI can transform finance by enabling faster, more accurate decision-making and giving finance professionals the ability to build custom tools to enhance their work. The article discusses the importance of AI in finance, emphasizing the need for meaningful work, cost-effectiveness, and efficiency, with the goal of creating a finance function that is AI-native and leads to faster cycles, better decisions, and more time for judgment. CFOs are encouraged to lead the AI transformation in finance to provide a working model for the entire company.
