summary: OpenAI is offering $5 million in funding grants for independent research on how generative AI affects teenagers aged 13-17. The research should focus on emotional and social development, patterns of AI use, and potential impacts on teens. Proposals must address ethical considerations and applicants must be affiliated with a research institution or have relevant experience. Grants can be up to $1 million per project, with a deadline of October 6, 2026. Selected proposals will be notified by November 13, 2026, and outputs should include a final report and recommendations for stakeholders.
