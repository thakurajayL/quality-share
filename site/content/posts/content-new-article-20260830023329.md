summary: Meta has developed MetaRoCE, a high-performance RDMA transport protocol for AI workloads on commodity Ethernet. They have released the specification, reference software implementation, and compliance test suite through the Open Compute Project (OCP) to encourage industry adoption. MetaRoCE aims to provide high throughput, low latency, and operational simplicity for AI infrastructure at scale by moving intelligence to the endpoint and optimizing network paths. The protocol has been validated for hardware acceleration and shown to outperform traditional RoCEv2. It is open-source, allowing for interoperability and innovation in AI infrastructure. Future work includes optimizing for scalability and storage/Kv-cache use cases. Meta is seeking collaboration with industry partners to further develop Ethernet for AI infrastructure.
