summary: OpenAI stresses the need for third-party assessments to ensure the safety and accountability of AI models. They support independent assessments across all stages of AI development, focusing on specific safety claims and cases. Four priority areas for assessment include safety cases, critical safeguards, capability evaluations, and investigation of misalignment incidents. Guidelines for safeguards and remediation are outlined to mitigate incidents in the future. The goal is to establish international standards for effective third-party assessments and grow the independent evaluation ecosystem. Multiple third parties are being engaged to align with these principles.
