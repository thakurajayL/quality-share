summary: Rebalancer, a resource allocation problem solver used by Meta for over nine years, has been open-sourced. It focuses on usability and scalability by separating problem specification from solution, offering a language for describing problems, and providing optimal and local search solvers. Rebalancer has been successfully used at Meta for infrastructure optimization and can handle millions of assignment problems daily. The tool includes the Rebalancer Explorer for debugging and the development team is continuously working to optimize performance and add new capabilities. Experts are encouraged to contribute to the project, which is available on GitHub, PyPI, and through documentation.
