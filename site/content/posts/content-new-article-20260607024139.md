summary: OpenAI is advocating for global action on youth AI safety by establishing a dedicated AI Safety Institute. They believe that AI can benefit young people, but it must be safe, age-appropriate, and supportive of healthy development. OpenAI is calling for collaboration at the G7 Leaders’ Summit to raise industry standards and establish an international youth safety institute. They have outlined principles for companies to ensure youth safety, including age-appropriate protections, regular risk assessments, parental controls, transparent safety policies, and protocols for addressing safety situations. OpenAI is committed to promoting AI literacy, protecting personal information, and enforcing accountability mechanisms for youth safety. They are working with governments and educators to deploy AI tools in education and convening the OpenAI Forum in Paris to discuss safeguards and standards for youth safety in AI.
