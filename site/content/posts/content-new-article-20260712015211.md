They have formed partnerships with multiple nations for cyber and biosecurity defense, with limitations on the application of their technology for mass surveillance and autonomous weapons. OpenAI advocates for legislation to implement safeguards for high-risk military AI applications and aims to provide information, rather than dictate decisions, on AI in government through democratic means.
