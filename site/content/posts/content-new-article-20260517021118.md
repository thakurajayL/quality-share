summary: Sea Limited is implementing Codex, an AI-assisted software development tool, to improve productivity and navigate complexity in engineering teams. Sea sees AI as a structural multiplier that allows engineers to focus on higher-level tasks. The company believes Southeast Asia is ideal for AI-native software development due to its complex problems. Sea is reconfiguring engineering teams to be more leveraged as AI agents handle operational execution work, leading to more iterative development cycles. The company is democratizing access to AI primitives through Codex Hackathon Series to accelerate AI-driven innovation in the region.
