summary: OpenAI's public policy agenda aims to ensure that artificial general intelligence (AGI) benefits humanity by prioritizing democratization, empowerment, universal prosperity, resilience, and adaptability. They engage with governments and civil society to shape policies related to AI safety, cybersecurity, teen safety, and protection against child sexual abuse. OpenAI advocates for strong safeguards, transparency, accountability, and international standards to address the risks and opportunities of AI. They emphasize the importance of promoting AI literacy, providing access to AI tools, supporting workforce training, and protecting against harmful uses of AI. Collaboration with communities is crucial for sustainable AI infrastructure development and economic growth.
