summary: Meta has released RCCLX, an upgraded version of RCCL for AMD platforms, integrated with Torchcomms. It includes features like Direct Data Access and Low Precision Collectives for improved performance on AMD hardware, supporting FP32 and BF16 data types. RCCLX aims to facilitate easy adaptation of AI models across platforms and is compatible with Torchcomms API. Special thanks to the AMD RCCL team and Meta employees for their contributions.
