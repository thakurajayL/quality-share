summary: AI is changing the way people work by enabling task crossover, where individuals take on tasks from other occupations. This trend is evident in ChatGPT user messages, with a significant percentage of work-related messages involving tasks outside of the user's own occupation. This shift is particularly notable in customer experience, design, human resources, legal, and marketing occupations. AI is reshaping job roles and responsibilities, allowing for shared tasks across multiple occupations. The impact of AI on work varies based on the size and structure of a business, with larger companies having more specialized teams and workflows. Overall, AI serves as a generalist tool that enables workers to experiment with new combinations of activities before traditional labor-market statistics reflect these changes.
