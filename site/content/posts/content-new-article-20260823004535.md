summary: OpenAI offers Zero Data Retention to eligible API customers, promising not to retain prompts or model responses. Private Safety Processing is being previewed to identify patterns without accessing customer content. ZDR deployments allow customer content to remain on their infrastructure or encrypted on OpenAI's infrastructure. This approach aims to balance AI safety with customer control and privacy, especially for organizations handling sensitive information. Customers can appeal enforcement decisions and collaborate with OpenAI to investigate abuse. The rollout of Private Safety Processing and a technical white paper is planned for September.
